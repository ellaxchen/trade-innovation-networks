Trade & Innovation Networks

Two directed networks on the same countries — trade flows (BACI, 1995–2024) and patent citations (PATSTAT, through 2017) — built side by side to ask whether trading with a country causes you to build on its ideas, or the reverse.

Key findings

  • China's knowledge leads its trade by 5 years (r = 0.990) — the strongest cross-correlation in the data, after correcting the 2010 coverage artifact.
  • Three development pathways: knowledge-first (US, Japan), trade-first (Korea, Netherlands, Switzerland), and dual rise (China, Canada, Italy).
  • At sector level the picture reverses: within electronics and machinery, China's trade led its knowledge.
  • Family-level citations are canonical: counting inventions (not filings) raises cross-border citations from 11% to 40.5%.
